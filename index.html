Hi, my name is Sahitha!

Hello! I'm a first-year PhD student at the University of Southern California working with Maja Matarić in the Interaction Lab, supported by the NSF GRFP. My research lies at the intersection of Human-Robot Interaction (HRI), accessibility, and care.

Previously in my undergrad at Rice University, I worked with Dr. Vaibhav Unhelkar to study error recovery and trust in robots with caregivers. Through the DUB REU program, I worked with Dr. Maya Cakmak to develop a shared autonomy system using end-user programming to enable more personalized robot interactions.

I'm interested in personalization and long-term human–robot interaction (HRI) in care contexts. My research aims to account for stakeholder differences and sociocultural contexts, increase agency for users with long-term care needs, and design for long-term deployment. I take an interdisciplinary approach that draws on critical studies and participatory methods to center lived experience in the design of intelligent systems.
